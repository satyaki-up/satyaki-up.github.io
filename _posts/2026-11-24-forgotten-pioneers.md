Probably many that I missed.

* [1994] BPE by Gage [[paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/177910.177914)]  
* [1987] Dropout (dilution) by Sompolinsky [[paper](https://neurophysics.ucsd.edu/courses/physics_171/Sompolinsky_PhysicsToday.pdf)]  
* [1969] ReLU (adaptive threshold element) by Fukushima [[paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4082265)]  
* [1980] CNN (neocognitron) by Fukushima [[paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00344251)]  
* [1976] Backprop by Linnainmaa [[paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01931367)]  
* [1967] Neural network by Shun'ichi Amari [[paper](https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/amari1967.pdf)]  
* [1962] Gradient descent by Dreyfus [[paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022247X62900045?via%3Dihub)]  
* [1961] Kelley-Bryson optimal control gradient procedure  
* [1960] ADALINE and Widrow-Hoff learning rule (gradient descent) by Widrow [[paper](https://web.archive.org/web/20180714173310/http://www-isl.stanford.edu/~widrow/papers/t1960anadaptive.pdf)]  
* [1958] Perceptron by Rosenblatt [[paper](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~lreyzin/papers/rosenblatt58.pdf)]
