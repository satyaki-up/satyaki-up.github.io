title:  "Notes on Transformers (2017-2022)"
---
 
* [2022] [OPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01068): 175B. ReLU. AdamW with linear decay. Gradient predivide for numerical stability.  
* [2022] [GPT-NeoX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.06745): 20B. Attention and MLP in parallel. RoPE. AdamW with cosine decay. Custom tokenizer.  
* [2021] [GPT-J](https://arankomatsuzaki.wordpress.com/2021/06/04/gpt-j/): 6B. Attention and MLP in parallel. RoPE. GELU.  
* [2021] [Jurassic-1](https://www.ai21.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/jurassic-1-technical-details-and-evaluation.pdf): 178B. Higher width/depth ratio. Custom tokenizer. Batch size warmup.  
* [2020] [GPT-3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165): 175B. Alternating dense/sparse attention. Gradient clipping. AdamW with cosine decay. Batch size scaling. Sequence packing.  
* [2019] [GPT-2](https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf): 1.5B. (Pre) LayerNorm.  
* [2018] [GPT-1](https://cdn.openai.com/research-covers/language-unsupervised/language_understanding_paper.pdf): 117M. MHA. GELU. (Post) LayerNorm. Learned positional embeddings. AdamW with cosine decay.  
* [2017] [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762): 200M encoder-decoder. MHA. ReLU. (Post) LayerNorm. sqrt{d} scaling to sinusoidal positional embeddings. AdamW with sqrt decay.
