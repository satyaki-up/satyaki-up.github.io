It's the week before Anthropic is set to go public, which'll be the first major AI lab to do so. I've been following the AI trade for a few years now, and thought I'd write down my predictions for the next decade, similar to a [Bezos](https://www.reddit.com/r/Entrepreneur/comments/t7x5ko/whats_not_going_to_change_in_the_next_10_years/)-style question: What's not going to change in the next 10 years?  

I'll revisit my predictions in 10 years.  

**Do I believe in existential risk from AI?** No, I don't believe in x-risk and all the arguments I've seen for it, make jumps of logical reasoning that are tenuous. My p(doom) is close to 0.  

**Will there be a Singularity?** By that, I mean a single day after which the state of the world is different from any time before (similar to the first atomic bomb). No, I don't think so - I think change from AI will be gradual and will take years.  

**Will AI completely automate software engineering?** Yes, I think so but it'll happen over years.  

**Will AI completely automate all knowledge work?** I don't know - it's not obvious to me that it can be extrapolated directly from software engineering being automated. Even if this happens, it won't happen in the next decade.  

**Will AI cause mass unemployment?** No, AI will automate many jobs but it'll be over years or even decades, mainly because of frictions that're naturally present in society. Many new categories of jobs will be created.  

**How big is the market for AI?** I agree with Anthropic's claim that the TAM is the market of all white collar work, so atleast 40T as they mention in their S-1. It's likely that the TAM will increase further because historically, technological paradigm shifts tended to create new markets.  

**Is the AI model provider market a winner-take-all market?** No, I don't believe that because it's unlikely that the biggest market in history will be dominated by one player. Society itself won't allow it.  

**Are AI models going to be commoditized?** No, different AI models will be better at different things and there'll be a lot more variety in the market. AI in 2026 is like the Internet in 1996.  

**How much will AI labs be worth?** All technological revolutions start will a bubble and then there's a market correction. Over the long term (10-15 years), every successful AI lab will be worth atleast 1T.
