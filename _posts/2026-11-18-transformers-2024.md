* [2024] [Gemma 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118): 27B. 2B and 9B via KD. 13T tokens. Interleaved local-global attention. GQA. RoPE. GeGLU. Logit soft-capping for attention and final layer. RMSNorm both pre and post.  
* [2024] [Qwen 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10671): 72B dense and 57B-A14B MoE. 7T tokens (tried 12T but didn't make a difference). Same tokenizer as Qwen 1. GQA. Dual Chunk Attention with YARN for long context. RoPE (10k->1000K). QKV bias. RMSNorm. SwiGLU.  AdamW with cosine decay.  
* [2024] [Yi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04652):  
* [2024] [Mixtral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04088): 47B-A13B. Similar to 7B dense.  
* [2024] [Gemma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08295): 7B. 6T tokens. Same tokenizer as PaLM. MHA for bigger and MQA for smaller. RoPE. Tied embeddings. GeGLU. RMSNorm.  
* [2024] [DeepSeek](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02954): 67B. 2T tokens. Huggingface tokenizer. RMSNorm. SwiGLU. GQA. RoPE. Unlike most GQA models, expanded depth over width. AdamW with multi-step decay (found to be similar to cosine). HAI-LLM. ZeRO-1+TP+SP+PP. Scaling laws for LR/batch size based on FLOPs/token instead of params as measure of scale.
